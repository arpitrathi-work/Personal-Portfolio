FAQ

Questions people usually ask

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your price, goals and how fast you want to learn. We agree the budget together on the call based on scope and direction. It's paid directly to Meta, separate from my fee.

How soon will I see results?

The first few weeks are about learning: which hooks, audiences and offers respond. Some campaigns find traction quickly, others need more testing. I'll set clear expectations for your case before we start.

What access do you need?

Partner access to your ad account, Page and pixel or dataset through your business settings. You stay the owner of everything, and you can remove access at any time.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Results depend on your offer, price, sales page and market. What I can promise is careful testing, honest reporting and quick action on what the data shows.

Do you make the ad videos?

I write the scripts and hooks and plan the variations. You or your team record them, which keeps the ads authentic to your voice.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work with coaches, course creators and physiotherapy clinics in India and the US. Calls are scheduled to suit both time zones.
